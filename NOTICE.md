# Third-Party Media Notice

This repository contains a small set of speech-enhancement examples derived
from third-party research datasets. The audio is included only to demonstrate
Continual Residual Memory and remains subject to the applicable upstream terms.

- DNS examples are drawn from the [DNS Challenge 2020 synthetic no-reverberation test set](https://github.com/microsoft/DNS-Challenge/tree/interspeech2020/master). Its component speech and noise recordings retain their upstream licenses, including PTDB-TUG (ODbL 1.0), AudioSet (CC BY 4.0), and selected Freesound material (CC0), as documented by the DNS Challenge project.
- EARS-D examples use speech from the [EARS dataset](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ears_dataset), distributed under CC BY-NC 4.0, mixed with noise from [DEMAND](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1227121), distributed under CC BY 4.0.
- DNS transcripts are adapted from the community-maintained [DNS_text](https://github.com/Emrys365/DNS_text) project, distributed under Apache-2.0. EARS transcripts come from the official EARS annotations.

The repository-level GPL-3.0 license applies to the website code and original
project material. It does not replace or supersede licenses attached to the
third-party audio and transcript sources listed above.
